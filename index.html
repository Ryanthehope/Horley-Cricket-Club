Welcome to Horley Cricket Club

Why Join Horley CC?

Cricket for All Ages & Abilities:

We pride ourselves on being a truly inclusive club. From our competitive Cherwell Cricket League Saturday sides to our relaxed Sunday Mixed XI, we offer opportunities for cricketers of all levels.

Thriving Junior Section: We are dedicated to nurturing the next generation of cricketers. Our youth programs focus on building skills, teamwork, and—most importantly—having fun in a safe environment.

Stunning Location: Our ground is widely regarded as one of the most beautiful in the county, providing a classic English village setting to play and watch the game.

More Than Just a Club: Beyond the boundary, Horley CC is a social hub. With our local roots and a welcoming clubhouse, we host regular events and welcome "associate members" who just want to be part of the local spirit.

Founded on a passion for the game and a commitment to our village, Horley Cricket Club is always looking for new players, volunteers, and supporters.

Ready to pad up? Come down to the ground, meet the team, and experience the "Little Club with the Big Welcome."

Our Sponsors

We are grateful for the support of our sponsors, who help us achieve our goals and promote cricket in our community.
